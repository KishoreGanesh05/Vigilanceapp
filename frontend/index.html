Process
How Sathya.Watch
Protects You

Built for real-world conditions in India. Designed so that even if someone seizes our servers, your evidence survives.

You file a report
No account or identity required. You describe the incident, name the official or department, and add evidence files.
Metadata is stripped
Before any file touches our servers, all EXIF data — GPS coordinates, device model, timestamp — is removed. Your location cannot be inferred from a photo.
File is hashed
A SHA-256 fingerprint of your original file is computed and stored. This proves the file existed at this exact moment and has not been altered since.
Evidence goes to IPFS
Files are uploaded to the InterPlanetary File System — a decentralized network. No single server or court order can delete them. The IPFS CID is your proof.
You get a token
A random 32-character token is the only link between you and your report. No email, no phone, no name. Keep it safe.
Escalation (coming)
Reviewed reports are escalated to India Lokayukta, CVC, or partner NGOs. You will be notified via your token.
Who reviews reports?

Currently reports are reviewed by the platform moderator. We are in the process of partnering with India anti-corruption NGOs and legal aid organizations. Verified journalists can apply for access to the public report feed (identity-verified, report content is anonymized).
